# SEO and GEO Report

## Executive summary

Current estimated score: SEO 72/100 and GEO 58/100. The site is technically clean and semantically understandable, but it has limited indexable depth, no project detail pages, no evidence-rich case study content, and no confirmed external authority signals. The enhanced branch raises the implementation baseline; rankings still depend on publishing, links, and verified business/profile data.

## Recommended keyword set

Primary: `software engineer Paris`, `full stack software engineer Paris`, `product-minded software engineer`, `software engineer AI products`.

Secondary: `React Native developer Paris`, `backend engineer blockchain`, `mobile product engineer`, `AI product builder`, `TypeScript Node.js developer`, `software engineer portfolio Paris`.

Long-tail: `software engineer building web and AI products in Paris`, `React Native and Node.js product engineer`, `blockchain asset platform developer`, `software engineer open to relocation Europe`.

Use one primary intent per page. Keep the homepage focused on the person and role; create separate project pages for Cachet, Quant Market Prediction, and Automated Trading Bot rather than repeating every keyword on one page.

## SEO findings

- Strong: descriptive title, canonical URL, robots directive, sitemap, semantic sections, crawlable HTML, downloadable CV, and stable internal anchors.
- Strong after enhancement: Person JSON-LD, Open Graph metadata, author metadata, mobile-safe viewport, and clearer entity language.
- Gap: add a real `og:image`, a favicon/manifest strategy for installed mobile use, and a visible “last updated” date when content changes.
- Gap: create project detail pages with problem, role, stack, outcome, screenshots, links, and dates. This is the largest content opportunity.
- Gap: validate the canonical domain, submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and check the site with Lighthouse and Rich Results Test.
- Gap: do not claim quantified outcomes unless you can substantiate them.

## GEO readiness

GEO here means making the site easy for answer engines and assistants to identify, quote, and recommend. The current homepage has a clear entity, role, location, skills, experience, and social profiles. Its GEO weakness is evidence density: assistants have little structured project context to cite beyond short summaries.

Recommended actions:

1. Add concise, factual project case studies with explicit questions as headings: “What was Cachet?”, “What did Leonard own?”, and “What technologies were used?”.
2. Add an About page or expanded section with a stable one-sentence identity statement, location, current availability, and preferred roles.
3. Keep LinkedIn, GitHub, CV, and website facts consistent, especially dates and job titles.
4. Add Organization/Person relationships only where accurate; do not add fake reviews, awards, or employer claims.
5. Publish a small technical note or project write-up occasionally so external pages can corroborate expertise.

## Grade

SEO: **72/100 today; 82/100 after the implemented metadata and sitemap changes are deployed and validated.**

GEO: **58/100 today; 72/100 after project case studies, consistent external profiles, and corroborating technical content are published.**

These are heuristic implementation/content grades, not a prediction of ranking position.

## Measurement plan

Track indexed pages, branded queries, non-branded impressions, click-through rate, branded knowledge-panel/entity accuracy, and recruiter conversion from the contact CTA. Review after 30 and 90 days in Search Console and analytics.
