A wedding website built around their story.

A bespoke guest experience designed and built from scratch with the couple, then published as a live website for their wedding.

The brief 01

Useful for guests.
Personal to them.

The couple needed one place where guests could find the practical details of the day while still feeling the character of the people getting married.

We co-designed the direction together, balancing their visual references and imagery with the information guests would actually need before the wedding.

What I built 02

One clear place
for the day.

01 / Guests

RSVP to Excel

Guest responses were sent to an Excel sheet, keeping the information accessible to non-technical people instead of putting it behind a database dashboard.

02 / Information

Schedule and details

The site brought together the schedule, countdown, dress code, and essential guest information in a format designed to be useful on a phone.

03 / Location

Venue map

An OpenStreetMap-based venue map gave guests a functioning way to understand where they needed to go without adding unnecessary complexity.

The takeaway 03

Good client work
starts with listening.

The interesting part was not only building the features. It was translating a couple’s visual identity into a digital experience while keeping every practical detail easy to find.

That balance—personal expression, clear information, and a product people can actually use—is the kind of work I want to keep doing.
